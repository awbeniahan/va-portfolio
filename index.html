  • Client Task Board & Team Chat | Trello + Slack

    A task board and team chat for a fictional store owner, with urgent items easy to spot.

    Problem
    A fictional apparel store owner has urgent items (failed payments, a chargeback, a supplier delay) mixed in with routine customer, supplier and marketing tasks, with no clear view of what needs action first.
    What I did
    • Built a Trello board with 4 lists (To Do, In Progress, Waiting on Others, Done) and 12 task cards drawn from a mock inbox.
    • Color-coded the cards with 5 labels: Urgent, Customer Support, Orders & Fulfillment, Suppliers & Vendors and Marketing & Collabs. A supplier delay carries both Urgent and Suppliers & Vendors.
    • Added due dates and step-by-step checklists to the 4 urgent cards.
    • Set up one Trello automation rule: when a card is moved to Done, it is marked complete.
    • Set up a Slack workspace with 4 channels (#urgent, #orders, #suppliers, #customer-support) and posted sample messages, with urgent updates in #urgent each linked to its Trello card.
    • Connected Trello to Slack so a shared card appears in the channel as a preview with its label, checklist progress and due date.
    • Scheduled a weekday Slack reminder at 10 PM (UTC+8) to check the Urgent cards.
    Result
    A 12-card board where filtering by Urgent shows only the 4 tasks that need action, and a Slack workspace where urgent updates link straight to their cards.
